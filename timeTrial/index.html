TIME TRIAL

!!!!!!!!! READ ME READ ME READ ME READ ME !!!!!!!!!!!!!

Every goal you complete adds time to your clock. When your clock runs out, your run is over. The last one standing wins, so First to complete = Last.

  • Set the category to something like High% or AA, so the timer does not stop when someone kills the dragon.
  • Make sure there are enough Completions for everyone in the room, and enable Disable reset.
